What types of commercial properties does PPC service?

PPC LLC serves commercial pool and spa environments for resorts, hospitality properties, apartments, multifamily communities, HOAs, municipal facilities, public facilities, and other commercial properties in Southern Nevada.

Does PPC maintain both commercial pools and spas?

Yes. PPC LLC supports commercial pools and commercial spas with scheduled maintenance, chemistry attention, cleaning routines, filtration observations, and issue reporting.

Does PPC offer emergency pool and spa service?

PPC LLC can review urgent pool and spa service requests. Availability, timing, and scope must be confirmed directly before service is scheduled.

Can PPC assist with biological contamination incidents?

Yes. PPC LLC provides emergency bio cleanup support for contamination incidents, including treatment, recovery procedures, chemistry recovery, filtration support, and operational checks. PPC does not guarantee reopening time.

Does PPC support SNHD inspection readiness?

Yes. PPC LLC supports SNHD inspection readiness through maintenance routines, water chemistry attention, equipment observations, and communication with property teams. PPC does not guarantee inspection approval or regulatory compliance.
