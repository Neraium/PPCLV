Commercial Services

Pool, spa, equipment, emergency, and SNHD inspection-readiness support.

Detailed service support for commercial aquatic environments in Las Vegas and Southern Nevada.

Careful Scope PPC supports readiness and recovery work without guaranteeing regulatory approval, reopening time, or compliance outcomes.
01

Commercial Pool Maintenance

Routine commercial pool care for high-use facilities. PPC focuses on water condition, presentation, filtration, equipment observations, and useful reporting for property teams.

  • Water chemistry testing and balancing
  • Filtration checks
  • Skimming, brushing, vacuuming, and tile-line care
  • Chemical feeder observations
  • Pressure and circulation checks
  • Property-team reporting
02

Commercial Spa Maintenance

Commercial spas change quickly because heat and bather load increase sanitizer demand. PPC supports water quality, filtration, circulation, drain needs, and surface presentation.

  • High-temperature sanitizer demand review
  • Frequent water-quality checks
  • Drain and refill support
  • Filter maintenance
  • Jet and circulation checks
  • Surface and presentation care
03

Equipment Repair and Troubleshooting

PPC reviews core pool and spa equipment when clarity, circulation, heating, chemical delivery, or access is affected. Observations are communicated in practical terms for property contacts.

  • Pumps and motors
  • Filters
  • Valves and plumbing
  • Heaters
  • Chemical feeders
  • Controllers and automation observations
04

Acid Washing and Surface Restoration

Surface restoration supports pools and spas affected by scale, staining, or buildup. Work may include preparation, treatment, cleanup, refill, and chemistry recovery.

  • Scale and staining assessment
  • Drain preparation
  • Surface treatment
  • Rinse and cleanup
  • Refill and chemistry recovery
05

Emergency Service

Urgent issues can affect access, safety, water quality, equipment, or operations. PPC reviews the issue, coordinates next steps, and communicates scope without guaranteeing response time or reopening.

  • Circulation problems
  • Water-quality incidents
  • Equipment disruptions
  • Unexpected closures
  • Communication with property teams
06

Emergency Bio Cleanup

Bio cleanup support is available for contamination incidents that require treatment and recovery procedures. PPC supports chemistry recovery and operational checks without guaranteeing reopening time.

  • Contamination response
  • Treatment and circulation procedures
  • Filtration support
  • Chemistry recovery
  • Operational verification before return to service
07

Health Department Compliance Support

PPC supports inspection readiness through consistent maintenance attention and issue reporting. Property owners and operators remain responsible for regulatory obligations.

  • Inspection-readiness routines
  • Record and condition awareness
  • Chemistry consistency
  • Equipment observations
  • Clear issue reporting
