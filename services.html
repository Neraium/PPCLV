Commercial Services

Commercial pool and spa services for Greater Las Vegas.

PPC provides commercial pool and spa service for resorts, hospitality properties, communities, aquatic facilities, and large private estates across the Greater Las Vegas Area.

Routine maintenance, practical equipment support, surface care, and direct operational coordination in one commercial service relationship.

Maintenance

Consistent care for commercial water.

Commercial Pool Maintenance

Routine care focused on water condition, cleanliness, filtration, circulation, equipment observations, and clear updates for the property team.

Commercial Spa Maintenance

Commercial spa care that accounts for heat, bather load, sanitizer demand, filtration, circulation, and surface presentation.

Equipment & Restoration

Practical support for systems and surfaces.

Equipment Repair & Troubleshooting

PPC reviews equipment concerns affecting circulation, filtration, heating, chemical delivery, or facility operation and communicates practical next steps.

Chemical Feed & Automation Support

Observation and troubleshooting support for chemical feeders, controllers, and automation interfaces, with clear reporting when settings, delivery, or equipment condition need attention.

Acid Washing & Surface Restoration

Planned surface care for commercial pools and spas affected by scale, staining, or buildup, with attention to treatment and recovery.

Pool Deck Cleaning

Commercial pool deck power washing using cleaning solutions selected for compatibility with pool areas, deck surfaces, and surrounding décor, focused on helping remove dirt, buildup, organic debris, and surface staining. Surface material and condition affect compatibility and results, and complete stain removal is not promised.

Urgent & Operational Support

Clear coordination when operations are affected.

Emergency Service & Bio Cleanup

Support for urgent water-quality, circulation, equipment, or biological contamination concerns, including water care, filtration attention, recovery coordination, and clear property-team communication, subject to service availability.

Certified Pool Operator (CPO) Services

Professional Pool Care LLC provides Certified Pool Operator coverage for commercial aquatic facilities that require qualified operational oversight, routine documentation, water-quality management, and support for applicable health and safety requirements. Service can include routine operational checks, chemistry and equipment observations, service logs, inspection-readiness support, and on-site coverage where required by the facility.

Inspection-Readiness & Compliance Support

Maintenance attention, equipment observations, documentation, and practical support to help property teams prepare for applicable health and safety reviews.

PPC supports maintenance and inspection readiness. Property owners and operators remain responsible for applicable regulatory requirements.

Large Private Estates

Pool and spa support for large private estates.

Large private estates can receive applicable PPC services including water care, equipment support, chemical automation, spa service, pool deck cleaning, and other services based on the property's needs. The right service scope depends on the estate's aquatic facilities and operating needs.
