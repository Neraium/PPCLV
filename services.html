Commercial Services

Commercial pool and spa services for Greater Las Vegas.

PPC serves resorts, hospitality properties, communities, commercial aquatic facilities, and large private estates throughout the Greater Las Vegas Area.

Qualified operational coverage, documented service, equipment support, and responsive coordination for commercial aquatic facilities, all through one service relationship.

Maintenance

Consistent care for commercial pools and spas.

Commercial Pool Maintenance

Routine care covering water quality, cleanliness, filtration, circulation, equipment observations, and clear service reporting.

Commercial Spa Maintenance

Spa service accounts for heat, bather load, sanitizer demand, filtration, circulation, and surface presentation.

Equipment & Restoration

Equipment, automation, and surface care.

Equipment Repair & Troubleshooting

PPC assesses equipment concerns affecting circulation, filtration, heating, chemical delivery, or facility operation, then outlines findings and next steps.

Chemical Feed & Automation Support

PPC reviews chemical feeders, controllers, and automation interfaces, including settings, delivery, and equipment condition, with clear reporting on concerns and next steps.

Acid Washing & Surface Restoration

Planned treatment for pool and spa surfaces affected by scale, staining, or buildup, with scope and recovery steps based on surface condition.

Pool Deck Cleaning

Commercial pool deck power washing uses cleaning solutions selected for compatibility with pool areas, deck surfaces, and surrounding décor. Surface material and condition affect compatibility and results; complete stain removal is not guaranteed.

Urgent & Operational Support

Clear coordination when operations are affected.

Emergency Service & Bio Cleanup

PPC responds to urgent water-quality, circulation, equipment, and biological contamination concerns with appropriate water care, filtration attention, recovery coordination, and clear communication, subject to service availability.

Certified Pool Operator (CPO) Services

PPC provides Certified Pool Operator (CPO) coverage for facilities that need qualified operational oversight. Service may include routine documentation, water-quality management, chemistry and equipment observations, operational checks, service logs, inspection-readiness assistance, and on-site coverage where required by the facility.

Inspection-Readiness & Compliance Support

Maintenance, equipment observations, and documentation help owners and operators prepare for applicable health and safety reviews.

PPC supports maintenance and inspection readiness. Property owners and operators remain responsible for applicable regulatory requirements.

Large Private Estates

Pool and spa support for large private estates.

Large private estates can receive a tailored service scope covering water care, equipment troubleshooting, chemical automation, spa service, and pool deck cleaning. Additional work is evaluated according to the property's aquatic facilities and operating needs.
