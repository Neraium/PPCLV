About PPC LLC

Commercial pool care grounded in the needs of the property.

Professional Pool Care LLC, also known as PPC LLC, is family owned and operated in Las Vegas and has served commercial pools and spas since 2003.

Commercial Focus

A service relationship for managed aquatic facilities.

PPC works with the people responsible for commercial pools, spas, equipment rooms, and shared aquatic spaces.

The general maintenance approach considers water condition, circulation, filtration, equipment observations, surface presentation, and the operating priorities of the property.

Service is focused across the Greater Las Vegas Area.

How PPC Works

Practical care and clear communication.

Facility awarenessCommercial service is approached with property access, guest or resident use, and day-to-day operations in mind.

Maintenance rhythmRoutine attention supports water quality, equipment condition, presentation, and useful service records.

Property-team updatesCommunication is framed for managers, boards, engineers, and facility operators who need clear next steps.

Regional focusLas Vegas heat, dust, bather load, and seasonal use influence the needs of commercial aquatic facilities.
