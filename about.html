About PPC LLC

Commercial pool care grounded in the needs of the property.

Family owned and operated in Las Vegas since 2003, PPC serves commercial aquatic facilities, resorts, hospitality properties, communities, and large private estates throughout the Greater Las Vegas Area.

Commercial Focus

Built around the way commercial pools and spas actually operate.

PPC works with the people responsible for commercial pools, spas, equipment rooms, shared aquatic spaces, and complex pool and spa systems at large private estates.

Service considers water quality, circulation, filtration, equipment condition, presentation, and the day-to-day operating priorities of the property.

PPC serves properties throughout the Greater Las Vegas Area. Explore PPC's commercial pool and spa services to learn more about available support.
